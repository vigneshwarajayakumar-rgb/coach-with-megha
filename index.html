FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Your plan can be built for a full gym, a home setup with a few dumbbells, or bodyweight only.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Every plan starts from where you are today, and I'll guide you through each exercise step by step.

Will I have to follow a strict diet?

No crash diets and no banned foods. You'll learn how to eat for your goals using the foods you already enjoy.

How does online coaching work?

After you join, you'll receive your personalised plan, then check in with me every week. We stay in touch on WhatsApp in between, and I adjust your plan as you progress.

What's the difference between 1 on 1 and in-person coaching?

1 on 1 coaching is online, so you can join from anywhere. It's a 2-month program with a personalised diet plan, 8 private sessions where I demonstrate and correct your form, a custom workout plan for your specific goal (gym or home) and a personalised tracker. In-person coaching is my premium option for clients in Coimbatore: you train face to face with me, 3 days a week. Message me on WhatsApp for pricing and availability.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the program and duration you choose. Fill in the joining form or message me on WhatsApp and I'll share the details.
